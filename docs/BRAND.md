# Trapnest Zero

An immersive world within **Trapnest**, Henry's journal of Life & Art & Science & Technology. **Trapnest Theory** is a sibling project, featured in the map; it is not the parent identity of Zero.

The visual identity uses a folded elliptical loop in pearl paper and mint glass. The social artwork and transparent symbol were created with image generation. Navigation lockups, favicon variants, and an Apple touch icon use that symbol. Evergreen typography and generous space connect the assets to the existing mint, paper, and glass scenes.

- `public/assets/brand/og_image.jpg`: 1200 × 630 social artwork.
- `public/assets/brand/logo.png`: transparent 512 × 512 symbol.
- `public/assets/brand/nav_logo.svg`, `nav_logo_white.svg`: self-contained navigation lockups.
- `public/assets/brand/favicon.svg`, `favicon.png`, `public/favicon.ico`: favicon formats.
- `public/assets/brand/apple-touch-icon.png`: 180 × 180 icon.
- `src/content/narrative.json`: editable scene copy and original atlas rectangles.
- `tools/build-brand-content.mjs`: rerunnable typesetting for scene text and personalized keepsake paper; run with the Vite dev server active.

All scene geometry, camera motion, timing, transitions, shaders, interaction physics, immersive videos, and audio sources retain the recovered behavior. Existing academic papers and chalkboard are scene props. New copy uses the same atlas regions, anchors, and reveal effects. The final map's editorial cards use journal imagery in place of the old recruiting content. Historical source attribution and recovery manifests remain under `research/`.
